# Sleep-Promoting Foods

## Overview
Sleep-promoting foods supply precursors to sleep-regulating neurotransmitters (melatonin, serotonin, GABA) and contain minerals that calm the nervous system. They support the body's natural circadian rhythm without relying on pharmaceutical sleep aids.

## Key Foods

| Food | Key Nutrients | Primary Benefit | How to Prepare | Ayurvedic Note (Dosha Effect) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tart Montmorency cherries | Melatonin, anthocyanins, tryptophan | Natural melatonin source; improves sleep duration | Drink 1oz concentrate 1h before bed or eat whole | Cooling — balances Pitta and Vata |
| Turkey breast | Tryptophan, B6, zinc | Tryptophan converts to serotonin then melatonin | Roast or bake; avoid frying which reduces tryptophan | Neutral — balances all doshas in moderation |
| Warm milk (grass-fed) | Tryptophan, calcium, DHA | Calcium aids tryptophan conversion to melatonin | Warm gently with turmeric and nutmeg; do not boil | Calming for all doshas; best with warming spices for Vata |
| Pumpkin seeds | Magnesium, zinc, tryptophan | Magnesium relaxes muscles and calms nervous system | Eat raw or lightly roasted; 1-2oz before bed | Warming — balances Vata and Kapha |
| Almonds | Magnesium, melatonin, vitamin E | Supports sleep onset and quality | Soak overnight; eat 10-15 almonds before bed | Balances Vata and Pitta; may increase Kapha |
| Chamomile tea | Apigenin, bisabolol | Apigenin binds GABA receptors, promoting calm | Steep 5-10 min covered; drink 30-60 min before bed | Cooling — balances Pitta, good for Vata anxiety |
| Kiwi fruit | Serotonin, folate, antioxidants | Shown to improve sleep onset, duration, and efficiency | Eat 2 kiwis 1 hour before bed; eat raw, not blended | Slightly sour — balances Pitta and Kapha |
| Fatty fish (salmon, sardines) | Vitamin D, DHA, B6 | Vitamin D deficiency linked to poor sleep quality | Bake or poach; eat at dinner, not right before bed | Cooling — balances Pitta, use in moderation for Kapha |
| Bananas | Potassium, magnesium, tryptophan | Muscle relaxation and neurotransmitter support | Eat ripe (with spots); slightly green has less tryptophan | Slightly sweet — balances Vata, may aggravate Kapha |
| Ashwagandha (in milk) | Withanolides, triethylene glycol | Lowers cortisol; GABA-mimetic action | Simmer 1/2 tsp root powder in warm milk 10 min | Warming — strongly balances Vata, use cautiously in Pitta |

## Preparation Tips
- Warm milk gently (160F max) — boiling destroys tryptophan and denatures proteins that aid sleep.
- Steep chamomile covered to trap volatile oils (apigenin and bisabolol) that evaporate when exposed to air.
- Avoid blending or heavily processing tart cherry juice — the fiber slows sugar absorption and prevents blood sugar spikes that disrupt sleep.
- Soak nuts and seeds to increase bioavailability of magnesium and zinc by reducing phytic acid.
- Eat sleep-promoting foods 1-2 hours before bed, not immediately — the body needs time to convert tryptophan to melatonin.

## When to Eat
Tart cherry juice is most effective consumed 60-90 minutes before desired sleep time. Magnesium-rich foods (pumpkin seeds, almonds) can be eaten as a small evening snack. Warm spiced milk is traditionally consumed 30 minutes before bed. Avoid heavy sleep-promoting meals within 2 hours of lying down to prevent acid reflux from disrupting sleep. In Ayurvedic practice, eating the largest meal at noon and a light, early dinner (by 7pm) supports natural sleep onset.

## Cautions
While tart cherry juice is natural, it contains sugar — those with blood sugar imbalances should opt for whole cherries instead. Alcohol, often used as a sleep aid, fragments sleep architecture and suppresses REM sleep despite inducing drowsiness. Caffeine has a half-life of 5-6 hours and should be avoided after 2pm; individual slow metabolizers may need to cut off earlier.
