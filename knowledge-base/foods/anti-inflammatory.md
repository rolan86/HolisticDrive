# Anti-Inflammatory Foods

## Overview
Anti-inflammatory foods reduce chronic low-grade inflammation, which is a root driver of most modern diseases including arthritis, cardiovascular disease, and autoimmune conditions. These foods supply compounds that directly modulate the body's inflammatory pathways.

## Key Foods

| Food | Key Nutrients | Primary Benefit | How to Prepare | Ayurvedic Note (Dosha Effect) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Turmeric | Curcumin, turmerone | Inhibits COX-2 and NF-kB inflammatory pathways | Activate with black pepper and fats; avoid high heat | Warming — balances Kapha and Vata, aggravates Pitta in excess |
| Wild-caught salmon | EPA, DHA, astaxanthin | Reduces C-reactive protein and cytokines | Poach or bake below 350F to preserve omega-3s | Cooling — balances Pitta, use sparingly in Kapha |
| Ginger root | Gingerols, shogaols | Reduces prostaglandin production | Fresh in teas or stir-fries; peel minimally | Warming — balances Vata and Kapha, aggravates Pitta |
| Blueberries | Anthocyanins, quercetin | Lowers oxidative stress markers | Eat raw or lightly frozen; avoid prolonged cooking | Slightly cooling — balances Pitta, good for all doshas |
| Dark leafy greens | Vitamin K, chlorophyll, lutein | Neutralizes free radicals, supports cellular repair | Lightly steam or massage with oil for raw salads | Cooling — balances Pitta, may aggravate Vata if raw |
| Extra virgin olive oil | Oleocanthal, polyphenols | Mimics ibuprofen's anti-inflammatory mechanism | Use raw or low-heat cooking; do not fry | Balances all doshas in moderation |
| Bone broth | Collagen, glycine, glucosamine | Repairs intestinal inflammation and joint tissue | Simmer bones 12-24h with apple cider vinegar | Balances Vata, can increase Kapha if consumed heavily |

| Green tea | EGCG, L-theanine | Suppresses pro-inflammatory cytokines | Steep 3-5 min at 175F; avoid boiling water | Slightly astringent — balances Kapha and Pitta, Vata in moderation |

## Preparation Tips
- Cook turmeric with a fat source (ghee, coconut oil) and black pepper to increase curcumin absorption by up to 2000%.
- Steam rather than boil leafy greens to retain water-soluble vitamin C and folate.
- Avoid deep-frying omega-3 rich foods (salmon, walnuts) as high heat oxidizes fragile fatty acids.
- Use low-to-medium heat for olive oil; its polyphenols degrade above 375F.
- Soak nuts and seeds overnight in water with a pinch of salt to reduce anti-nutrients and improve digestibility.

## When to Eat
Anti-inflammatory foods are most effective when consumed consistently across all meals rather than in isolated bursts. Turmeric and ginger pair well with morning meals, while tart cherries and leafy greens suit lunch and dinner. Seasonal emphasis: prioritize berries in summer, root-based anti-inflammatory foods (turmeric, ginger) in winter.

## Cautions
Turmeric can interact with blood-thinning medications and may aggravate acid reflux in sensitive individuals. Nightshade vegetables (tomatoes, peppers, eggplant), while nutrient-dense, contain solanine which can worsen inflammation in people with autoimmune conditions or joint pain — monitor individual tolerance.
