# Hormone-Supporting Foods

## Overview
Hormone-supporting foods provide the raw materials for endocrine function and help modulate estrogen, progesterone, testosterone, cortisol, and thyroid hormones. They support the liver's detoxification pathways that process and eliminate excess hormones.

## Key Foods

|---|---|---|---|---|

| Avocado | Monounsaturated fats, B6, potassium | Supports cholesterol-based hormone synthesis | Eat ripe; add to salads or smoothies | Balances all doshas, especially Vata |
| Pomegranate | Ellagic acid, flavonoids | Inhibits aromatase, supporting balanced estrogen levels | Eat seeds fresh or juice; avoid added sugar | Cooling — balances Pitta, fine for all doshas |
| Pumpkin seeds | Zinc, magnesium, tryptophan | Zinc supports testosterone; magnesium aids progesterone | Eat raw or lightly roasted; soak 4-6h first | Warming — balances Vata, use moderately in Kapha |

## Preparation Tips
- Grind flaxseeds immediately before eating — whole flaxseeds pass through the digestive tract intact, preventing lignan absorption.
- Steam cruciferous vegetables lightly (5-7 minutes) rather than boiling or microwaving to preserve heat-sensitive indole-3-carbinol.
- Cook egg yolks softly (poached, soft-boiled) — overcooking oxidizes cholesterol and destroys heat-sensitive B vitamins and choline.
- Use gelatinized maca powder rather than raw maca, as gelatinization removes starch that can cause digestive upset.
- Soak pumpkin and sunflower seeds in water with salt for 4-6 hours, then dehydrate at low heat to improve mineral absorption.

## When to Eat
Flaxseeds and cruciferous vegetables are most effective consumed daily throughout the menstrual cycle. Maca is best taken in the morning as it can be slightly stimulating. Brazil nuts (2-3 per day) should be eaten in the first half of the day with a fat source for optimal selenium absorption. Seed cycling (flax/pumpkin in follicular phase, sesame/sunflower in luteal phase) aligns food timing with hormonal rhythms.

## Cautions
Soy products contain phytoestrogens that can be beneficial or disruptive depending on individual hormonal status — those with estrogen dominance should limit unfermented soy. Excessive iodine from kelp or seaweed supplements can trigger or worsen thyroid dysfunction; food-based sources are safer. Liver-supporting foods should be introduced gradually to avoid rapid estrogen detoxification that can temporarily worsen PMS symptoms.
