# Thyroid-Supporting Foods

## Overview
Thyroid-supporting foods provide essential nutrients for thyroid hormone production, conversion, and regulation—critical for metabolism, energy, and overall hormonal balance. These foods supply key minerals and compounds that support both thyroid function and autoimmune modulation.

## Preparation Tips
- Soak nuts and seeds overnight to reduce anti-nutrients and improve mineral absorption
- Lightly steam cruciferous vegetables (broccoli, kale) to neutralize goitrogens while preserving nutrients
- Cook with selenium-rich foods like Brazil nuts and mushrooms—selenium is heat-stable
- Pair iodine-rich foods with selenium sources for optimal thyroid hormone synthesis
- Use extra virgin coconut oil for low-to-medium heat cooking to preserve beneficial fatty acids

## When to Eat
Thyroid-supporting foods are best consumed in the morning and early afternoon when thyroid hormone production peaks. Include selenium and iodine sources daily, zinc-rich foods 3-4 times weekly. Seasonal focus: warming foods in colder months (cooked seaweed, warm bone broth), lighter preparations in summer.

## Cautions
Excess iodine from supplements (not food) can trigger hyperthyroidism in susceptible individuals. Those with autoimmune thyroid conditions (Hashimoto's) should moderate iodine intake and avoid large amounts of raw cruciferous vegetables—cooking neutralizes goitrogens effectively. Pregnant women need adequate iodine but should consult healthcare providers about supplementation.
