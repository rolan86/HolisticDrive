## Key Principles
- **Demulcents form a protective layer** — take Slippery Elm/Marshmallow 2 hours apart from medications
- **Bitters stimulate digestion** — Kutki, Chitrak best taken before meals
- **Carminatives relieve gas** — Peppermint, Ginger, Musta can be taken after meals
- **Triphala is foundational** — gentle enough for daily use, supports elimination
- **Enteric-coated peppermint** — prevents heartburn, targets lower GI tract

## Cautions
- Demulcents may interfere with medication absorption — separate by 2 hours
- Some gut herbs can aggravate GERD (Peppermint, Licorice) — use with caution
- Chitrak is very potent and heating — requires purification, avoid in pregnancy
- Neem is contraindicated in pregnancy — can be abortifacient
- Licorice can raise blood pressure with long-term use — consider DGL form
- Bitters may worsen dryness or deficiency conditions
