# Licorice (Glycyrrhiza glabra)

## Profile
- **Category:** Anti-inflammatory, Demulcent, Expectorant, Adrenal tonic
- **Ayurvedic:** Sweet rasa, cooling virya, sweet vipaka; pacifies Vata and Pitta, aggravates Kapha in excess; rejuvenative (rasayana)

## Evidence
- **Evidence level:** Moderate
- **Key studies:** Asl & Hosseinzadeh, Phytother Res, 2008 (comprehensive pharmacological review); Tu et al., J Gastroenterol Hepatol, 2015 (RCT, functional dyspepsia); Raveendra et al., J Ethnopharmacol, 2012 (RCT, oral mucositis)

## Key Compounds
- Glycyrrhizin (glycyrrhizic acid)
- Glycyrrhetic acid (glycyrrhetinic acid)
- Liquiritin
- Liquiritigenin
- Glabridin

## Dosing
- **Typical dose:** 200-400 mg deglycyrrhizinated licorice (DGL) or 1-2 g root powder, 2-3 times daily
- **Form:** Capsule, DGL chewable tablet, powder, decoction, tincture
- **Duration:** GI protective effects within 1-2 weeks; anti-inflammatory benefits at 4-6 weeks; limit glycyrrhizin-containing licorice to 4-6 weeks to avoid pseudoaldosteronism

## Cautions & Contraindications
- Avoid in hypertension, heart failure, and edema (glycyrrhizin causes sodium retention and potassium loss)
- Avoid in hypokalemia, liver cirrhosis, and renal impairment
- Contraindicated in pregnancy (risk of preterm birth)
- Use DGL form for long-term GI support (glycyrrhizin removed)
- Monitor blood pressure with regular licorice use beyond 2 weeks

## Drug Interactions
- Antihypertensives — may reduce efficacy due to sodium retention
- Cardiac glycosides (digoxin) — increased risk of toxicity with hypokalemia
- Diuretics (thiazides, loop diuretics) — increased potassium loss
- Corticosteroids — may potentiate effects and half-life
- Oral contraceptives and HRT — may increase hormone levels and side effects

## Key Sources
- Asl & Hosseinzadeh, "Review of Pharmacological Effects of Glycyrrhiza sp.," Phytother Res, 2008
- Isbrucker & Burdock, "Risk Assessment of Glycyrrhizin," Regul Toxicol Pharmacol, 2006
- Tu et al., "A Randomized Trial on Licorice for Dyspepsia," J Gastroenterol Hepatol, 2015
