# Astragalus (Astragalus membranaceus)

## Profile
- **Category:** Adaptogen / Immunomodulator
- **Ayurvedic:** Sweet rasa; warm virya; sweet vipaka; pacifies Vata, can aggravate Pitta and Kapha in excess

## Evidence
- **Evidence level:** Moderate
- **Key studies:** Cho WC, Leung KN. "In vitro and in vivo immunomodulating and immunorestorative effects of Astragalus membranaceus." Journal of Ethnopharmacology, 2007; Auyeung KK et al., American Journal of Chinese Medicine, 2016 (cardioprotective review); Van der Wielen N et al., Frontiers in Pharmacology, 2020 (polysaccharide mechanisms)

## Key Compounds
- Astragalosides (saponins) — cardioprotective, anti-inflammatory
- Astragalus polysaccharides (APS) — primary immunomodulators
- Calycosin (isoflavone) — antioxidant, estrogenic activity
- Formononetin — phytoestrogen, anti-inflammatory
- Beta-amyrin — antiulcer, hepatoprotective

## Dosing
- **Typical dose:** 1,000–3,000 mg of standardized root extract daily, divided into 2 doses
- **Form:** Capsule, decoction (traditional huang qi), tincture, or powder
- **Duration:** Immune and energy benefits typically within 4–8 weeks; safe for long-term use as a daily tonic

## Cautions & Contraindications
- Avoid in autoimmune conditions with hyperactive immunity (lupus, MS, rheumatoid arthritis) without clinical supervision
- Use with caution in patients on immunosuppressants — theoretical antagonism
- May have mild estrogenic activity — use cautiously in estrogen-sensitive conditions
- Pregnancy and lactation: avoid unless directed by a qualified practitioner
- Avoid in acute febrile illnesses per TCM tradition (tonic contraindicated during active infection)

## Drug Interactions
- Immunosuppressants (cyclosporine, tacrolimus, prednisone) — may reduce efficacy
- Anticoagulants/antiplatelets — possible additive effect, monitor INR
- Antihypertensives — may enhance blood pressure lowering
- Chemotherapy agents — may interact; coordinate with oncologist
- Estrogen-containing medications — potential additive estrogenic effect

- Auyeung KK, Han QB, Ko JK. "Astragalus membranaceus: a review of its protection against inflammation and cancer." American Journal of Chinese Medicine. 2016.
