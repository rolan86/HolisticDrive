# Echinacea (Echinacea purpurea)

## Profile
- **Category:** Immunomodulator, Anti-inflammatory, Antiviral
- **Ayurvedic:** Bitter, pungent, sweet rasa; cooling virya; sweet vipaka; pacifies Kapha, mildly aggravates Pitta

## Evidence
- **Evidence level:** Moderate
- **Key studies:** Karsch-Volk et al., Cochrane Database Syst Rev, 2014 (meta-analysis, common cold); Jawad et al., Virol J, 2012 (RCT, cold prevention); Block & Mead, Adv Ther, 2009 (RCT, Echinacea purpurea extract)

## Key Compounds
- Alkylamides (dodeca-2E,4E,8Z,10E/Z-tetraenoic acid isobutylamides)
- Chicoric acid
- Echinacoside
- Polysaccharides
- Flavonoids (quercetin, kaempferol)

## Dosing
- **Typical dose:** 300-500 mg standardized extract, 2-3 times daily at onset of symptoms
- **Form:** Capsule, tincture, tea, juice (pressed aerial parts)
- **Duration:** Take at first sign of cold for 7-10 days; may use prophylactically for up to 8 weeks; not recommended for continuous long-term use without breaks

## Cautions & Contraindications
- Avoid in autoimmune diseases (lupus, MS, RA) — immune-stimulating properties may exacerbate
- Avoid in progressive systemic diseases (tuberculosis, leukemia)
- Use cautiously in HIV/AIDS (controversial — some evidence supports use)
- Caution in severe allergies to asteraceae family (ragweed, daisies, marigolds)
- Generally not recommended in pregnancy (limited safety data)

## Drug Interactions
- Immunosuppressants (cyclosporine, methotrexate, corticosteroids) — may reduce efficacy
- Etoposide (chemotherapy) — may reduce drug clearance via CYP3A4
- Midazolam — may alter CYP3A4 metabolism
- Antifungals (ketoconazole) — potential CYP interaction
- Caffeine-containing drugs — may alter caffeine metabolism via CYP1A2

## Key Sources

- Barnes et al., "Echinacea purpurea: A Review," Lancet Infect Dis, 2005
- Manitto et al., "Metabolic Profiling of Echinacea," J Nat Prod, 2014
