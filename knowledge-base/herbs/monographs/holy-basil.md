# Holy Basil (Ocimum sanctum)

## Profile
- **Category:** Adaptogen, Nervine, Anti-inflammatory, Immunomodulator
- **Ayurvedic:** Pungent, bitter rasa; heating virya; pungent vipaka; pacifies Kapha and Vata, mildly aggravates Pitta

## Evidence
- **Evidence level:** Moderate
- **Key studies:** Saxena et al., J Ethnopharmacol, 2012 (RCT, stress and anxiety); Cohen et al., J Ayurveda Integr Med, 2014 (RCT, metabolic parameters); Jamshidi & Cohen, J Ethnopharmacol, 2017 (comprehensive review)

## Key Compounds
- Eugenol
- Ursolic acid
- Oleanolic acid
- Rosmarinic acid
- Linalool

## Dosing
- **Typical dose:** 300-600 mg standardized leaf extract, 1-2 times daily
- **Form:** Capsule, tincture, tea (fresh or dried leaves)
- **Duration:** Anxiolytic effects within 2-4 weeks; metabolic benefits at 8-12 weeks; safe for daily use as a tonic herb

## Cautions & Contraindications
- Avoid in pregnancy (uterine stimulant in animal studies)
- Use cautiously with hypothyroidism (may lower thyroid hormone levels)
- Caution in individuals on blood sugar-lowering medications
- May reduce fertility — avoid in couples trying to conceive
- Discontinue 2 weeks before surgery (blood sugar effects)

## Drug Interactions
- Antidiabetic medications (metformin, insulin) — enhanced blood sugar lowering, risk of hypoglycemia
- Thyroid hormone replacement (levothyroxine) — may interfere, monitor TSH
- Anticoagulants (warfarin) — mild antiplatelet effect, monitor INR
- Barbiturates and benzodiazepines — potential additive sedation
- Blood pressure medications — may have additive hypotensive effects
