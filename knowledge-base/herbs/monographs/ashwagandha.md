# Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera)

## Profile
- **Category:** Adaptogen, Nervine tonic, Anxiolytic
- **Ayurvedic:** Bitter/astringent rasa, heating virya, sweet vipaka; pacifies Vata and Kapha, aggravates Pitta in excess

## Evidence
- **Evidence level:** Strong
- **Key studies:** Chandrasekhar et al., Indian J Psychol Med, 2012 (RCT, 600 mg KSM-66 for stress); Lopresti et al., J Affect Disord, 2019 (RCT, sleep improvement); Salve et al., J Altern Complement Med, 2019 (systematic review of 12 RCTs)

## Key Compounds
- Withanolides (withaferin A, withanolide D)
- Withanoside IV and V
- Alkaloids (somniferine, withanine)
- Iron
- Steroidal lactones

## Dosing
- **Typical dose:** 300-600 mg standardized extract (2.5-5% withanolides), 1-2 times daily
- **Form:** Capsule, powder, tincture
- **Duration:** Anxiolytic effects within 2-4 weeks; full adaptogenic benefits at 8-12 weeks; safe for long-term use up to 1 year under supervision

## Cautions & Contraindications
- Avoid in pregnancy (uterine stimulant properties)
- Use cautiously in hyperthyroidism (may increase T3/T4)
- Caution in autoimmune conditions due to immune-stimulating effects
- Avoid in nightshade allergies (Solanaceae family)
- May cause mild GI upset at higher doses

## Drug Interactions
- Thyroid medications (levothyroxine) — additive effect, monitor thyroid levels
- Sedatives and benzodiazepines — additive CNS depression
- Immunosuppressants (cyclosporine, prednisone) — may reduce efficacy
- Antidiabetic medications — may enhance blood sugar lowering
- Blood pressure medications — may have additive hypotensive effects
