# Rhodiola (Rhodiola rosea)

## Profile
- **Category:** Adaptogen, Nervine tonic, Cognitive enhancer, Anti-fatigue
- **Ayurvedic:** Bitter, astringent rasa; neutral to slightly cooling virya; sweet vipaka; balancing to all three doshas when used appropriately

## Evidence
- **Evidence level:** Strong
- **Key studies:** Ishaque et al., Phytomedicine, 2012 (systematic review on stress); Darbinyan et al., Nord J Psychiatry, 2007 (RCT, depression); Hung et al., PLoS One, 2011 (meta-analysis on mental fatigue)

## Key Compounds
- Salidroside (rhodioloside)
- Rosavin
- Rosin
- Tyrosol
- Cinnamic alcohol glycosides

## Dosing
- **Typical dose:** 200-600 mg standardized extract (3% rosavins, 1% salidroside), 1-2 times daily
- **Form:** Capsule, tincture, powder
- **Duration:** Acute effects on fatigue within 30-60 minutes; full adaptogenic benefits at 4-8 weeks; cycle use (3-4 weeks on, 1-2 weeks off) recommended

## Cautions & Contraindications
- Avoid in bipolar disorder or mania (may be stimulating)
- Use cautiously in anxiety disorders with prominent agitation
- Caution in hypertension (may increase blood pressure at high doses)
- Avoid in pregnancy and lactation (insufficient safety data)
- May cause insomnia if taken too late in the day — take before 2 PM

## Drug Interactions
- Antidepressants (SSRIs, MAOIs) — theoretical risk of serotonin syndrome, use with caution
- Stimulants (caffeine, amphetamines) — additive CNS stimulation
- Antihypertensives — may reduce efficacy at high doses
- Antidiabetic medications — may affect blood sugar
- Immunosuppressants — immune-stimulating properties may reduce efficacy
