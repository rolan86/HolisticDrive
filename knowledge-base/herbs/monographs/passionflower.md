# Passionflower (Passiflora incarnata)

## Profile
- **Category:** Nervine, Anxiolytic, Mild sedative, Antispasmodic
- **Ayurvedic:** Bitter, sweet rasa; cooling virya; sweet vipaka; pacifies Pitta and Vata

## Evidence
- **Evidence level:** Moderate
- **Key studies:** Miyasaka et al., Phytother Res, 2007 (RCT, generalized anxiety); Ngan & Conduit, J Clin Pharm Ther, 2004 (systematic review, anxiety); Akhondzadeh et al., J Clin Pharm Ther, 2001 (RCT, opiate withdrawal)

## Key Compounds
- Chrysin
- Vitexin
- Orientin
- Passiflorine
- Harmala alkaloids (trace amounts)

## Dosing
- **Typical dose:** 250-500 mg standardized extract, 2-3 times daily; or 4-8 mL tincture (1:5), 3 times daily
- **Form:** Capsule, tincture, tea (infusion of dried aerial parts)
- **Duration:** Anxiolytic effects within 1-2 weeks; sleep benefits within 3-5 days; safe for intermediate-term use (up to 4 weeks without practitioner supervision)

## Cautions & Contraindications
- Avoid in pregnancy (uterine stimulant activity documented)
- Use cautiously in children under 6 (limited safety data)
- Caution when driving or operating machinery due to sedative effects
- Discontinue use before surgery (potential interaction with anesthetics)
- May cause drowsiness, dizziness, or confusion at high doses

## Drug Interactions
- Benzodiazepines and other sedatives — additive CNS depression
- MAOIs — harmala alkaloids may have weak MAOI activity
- Anticoagulants (warfarin) — theoretical interaction, monitor INR
- Barbiturates — additive sedative effects
- Alcohol — enhanced sedation and impairment
