# Marshmallow Root (Althaea officinalis)

## Profile
- **Category:** Gut herb / Demulcent
- **Ayurvedic:** Sweet rasa; cool virya; sweet vipaka; pacifies Pitta and Vata, may aggravate Kapha in excess

## Evidence
- **Evidence level:** Low to Moderate
- **Key studies:** Deters A et al., Phytomedicine, 2010 (mucilage anti-inflammatory in respiratory tract); White LM et al., Annals of Internal Medicine, 2001 (herbal therapy review including demulcents); Benavente-Garcia O et al., Journal of Agricultural and Food Chemistry, 2005 (phenolic composition and activity)

## Key Compounds
- Mucilage (rhamnogalacturonan polysaccharides) — primary demulcent
- Pectin — soothing, protective barrier
- Flavonoids (quercetin, kaempferol derivatives) — antioxidant, anti-inflammatory
- Coumarins — mild anti-inflammatory
- Scopoletin — antispasmodic, anti-inflammatory

## Dosing
- **Typical dose:** 400–500 mg of dried root extract 3 times daily; or 2–5 g of powdered root as a cold maceration (tea)
- **Form:** Cold infusion (preferred to preserve mucilage), capsule, tincture, powder
- **Duration:** Symptomatic relief typically within hours to days; safe for 4–8 weeks of continuous use; limited long-term data

## Cautions & Contraindications
- Take 2 hours apart from all other oral medications — mucilage significantly impedes absorption
- Avoid in known allergy to marshmallow or Malvaceae family plants
- Use with caution in diabetes — may slow glucose absorption and alter blood sugar readings
- May increase mucus production — avoid in excessive Kapha conditions per Ayurvedic tradition
- Pregnancy and lactation: likely safe in food amounts; insufficient data for therapeutic doses

## Drug Interactions
- All oral medications — mucilage forms a gel layer reducing absorption of concurrently administered drugs
- Antidiabetic agents — may enhance hypoglycemic effect through slowed carbohydrate absorption
- Topical corticosteroids — may enhance local anti-inflammatory effect (synergistic, not adverse)
- Lithium — possible decreased absorption
