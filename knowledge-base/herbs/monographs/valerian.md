# Valerian (Valeriana officinalis)

## Profile
- **Category:** Nervine, Sedative-hypnotic, Anxiolytic, Antispasmodic
- **Ayurvedic:** Bitter, pungent, astringent rasa; heating virya; pungent vipaka; pacifies Vata, mildly aggravates Pitta

## Evidence
- **Evidence level:** Moderate
- **Key studies:** Bent et al., Am J Med, 2006 (meta-analysis, sleep quality); Fernandez-San-Martin et al., BMC Med, 2010 (RCT, insomnia); Crocker, Am J Health Syst Pharm, 2010 (clinical review)

## Key Compounds
- Valerenic acid
- Valerenol
- Valepotriates
- Isovaleric acid
- Linalool

## Dosing
- **Typical dose:** 300-600 mg standardized extract (0.8% valerenic acid), 30-60 minutes before bed
- **Form:** Capsule, tincture, tea (decoction of root), tablet
- **Duration:** Sedative effects within 1-2 hours; improved sleep quality after 2-4 weeks of nightly use; safe for short-term use (2-4 weeks); long-term safety data limited

## Cautions & Contraindications
- Avoid in pregnancy and lactation (insufficient safety data)
- Use cautiously with liver disease (rare hepatotoxicity reports)
- Caution when driving or operating machinery due to sedation
- Paradoxical stimulation may occur in some individuals (restlessness, palpitations)
- Not recommended for children under 3 years of age

## Drug Interactions
- Benzodiazepines and other sedatives (zolpidem, eszopiclone) — additive CNS depression
- Barbiturates — enhanced sedative effects
- Alcohol — significantly increased sedation and impairment
- Antidepressants — may compound drowsiness
- CYP450 substrates — valerenic acid may inhibit CYP3A4, altering drug metabolism
