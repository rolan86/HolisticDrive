## Key Principles
- **Start low, go slow** — adaptogens work cumulatively over 4-12 weeks
- **Cycle periodically** — 6-8 weeks on, 1-2 weeks off to maintain sensitivity
- **Timing matters** — most adaptogens best taken in the morning (except Ashwagandha for sleep)
- **Consistency over intensity** — regular moderate doses outperform intermittent high doses
- **Individual response varies** — what works for one person may not work for another

## Cautions
- Many adaptogens stimulate the immune system — use cautiously in autoimmune conditions
- Some have hormonal effects (Ashwagandha, Shatavari, Holy Basil) — monitor in hormone-sensitive conditions
- May interact with thyroid, diabetes, and blood pressure medications
- Not all adaptogens are safe in pregnancy — always check individual monographs
- Consult healthcare provider before combining with prescription medications
