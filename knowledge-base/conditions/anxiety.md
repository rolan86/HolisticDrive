# Anxiety

## Holistic Perspective
Anxiety is understood holistically as a state of nervous system dysregulation in which the sympathetic "fight-or-flight" response becomes the default operating mode rather than an occasional adaptive response. While psychological factors are significant, anxiety also manifests from gut microbiome imbalances, blood sugar instability, chronic inflammation, nutrient deficiencies, hormonal shifts, and unresolved trauma stored in the body. A holistic approach addresses the physiological terrain alongside the psychological, recognizing that calming the body is often the prerequisite for calming the mind.

## Common Patterns
- Physical manifestations: rapid heartbeat, shallow breathing, muscle tension (especially jaw and shoulders), digestive upset, sweating, dizziness
- Co-occurring: insomnia, IBS, adrenal dysregulation, chronic pain, hypervigilance, perfectionism, people-pleasing
- Lab markers: low GABA or serotonin (neurotransmitter testing), elevated cortisol (especially evening), low magnesium RBC, low B12, low vitamin D, elevated homocysteine, elevated hs-CRP, gut dysbiosis on stool analysis
- Body systems: nervous system (central and enteric), endocrine system (HPA axis), digestive system, cardiovascular system, respiratory system

## Holistic Approaches

### Nutrition
- Stabilize blood sugar -- anxiety frequently spikes 2-3 hours after sugar or refined carbohydrate intake; pair all carbs with protein and fat
- Increase magnesium-rich foods: dark leafy greens (daily), dark chocolate (70%+, 1-2 squares), pumpkin seeds, almonds, avocados
- Omega-3 fatty acids: 2-4g EPA/DHA daily from wild-caught salmon, sardines, or high-quality fish oil supplement; strong anti-anxiety evidence
- Fermented foods daily for gut-brain axis support: sauerkraut, kimchi, coconut yogurt, miso (Lactobacillus and Bifidobacterium strains produce GABA)
- Foods rich in B vitamins: nutritional yeast (1-2 tbsp daily), pastured eggs, organ meats, legumes
- Chamomile tea (2-3 cups daily) -- apigenin content binds GABA receptors; stronger than placebo in GAD trials
- Green tea (1-3 cups daily) -- L-theanine content (200 mg) promotes alpha brain waves and calm focus without drowsiness
- Reduce or eliminate caffeine; even decaf coffee triggers anxiety in sensitive individuals due to other stimulant compounds

### Herbal Medicine
- **Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera):** 300-600 mg KSM-66 extract twice daily; reduces cortisol and anxiety scores by 30-50% in clinical trials
- **Passionflower (Passiflora incarnata):** 250-500 mg extract twice daily; increases GABA levels; comparable efficacy to oxazepam in trials
- **Lemon Balm (Melissa officinalis):** 300-600 mg extract or 2-4 mL tincture; combined with valerian for enhanced calming; avoid with hypothyroidism
- **Lavender (Lavandula angustifolia):** Silexan 80 mg capsule daily; clinically shown to reduce anxiety scores with comparable efficacy to SSRIs and benzodiazepines
- **Holy Basil / Tulsi (Ocimum sanctum):** 300-600 mg extract twice daily or 2-3 cups tea; adaptogenic cortisol modulator with anxiolytic properties
- **Kava (Piper methysticum):** 150-300 mg kavalactones for acute anxiety; strong evidence but rare hepatotoxicity risk -- short-term use only under supervision
- **Rhodiola (Rhodiola rosea):** 200-400 mg standardized to 3% rosavins in the morning; for anxiety with fatigue and burnout
- **CBD (Cannabidiol):** 25-50 mg broad-spectrum daily; 300-600 mg shown effective for acute public speaking anxiety in clinical trials

### Ayurvedic Perspective
- **Vata** anxiety (racing thoughts, fear, insomnia, constipation): grounding and warming practices; warm sesame oil Abhyanga daily, warm spiced milk at bedtime, grounding root vegetables, regular routine
- **Pitta** anxiety (irritability, anger, acid reflux, competitiveness): cooling practices; coconut oil massage, brahmi oil on temples and scalp, bitters (aloe vera juice, amla), moonlight walks
- **Kapha** anxiety (lethargy, worry, attachment, excessive sleep): stimulating practices; dry brushing, vigorous pranayama (Kapalabhati), trikatu spice mix, early rising
- **Shirodhara** (warm oil stream on forehead) profoundly calms Vata and Pitta anxiety; one of the most effective Ayurvedic treatments
- **Brahmi (Bacopa monnieri):** 300-500 mg daily; medhya rasayana (brain tonic) that enhances GABA and reduces anxiety over 8-12 weeks
- **Jatamansi (Nardostachys jatamansi):** Ayurvedic nervine sedative; 300-500 mg or 1 tsp in warm milk at bedtime; Ayurvedic equivalent of valerian
- **Saraswatarishta** (10-20 mL with equal water after meals): classical Ayurvedic nervine tonic containing brahmi, shatavari, and asparagus

### Movement & Exercise
- **Yoga** is one of the most evidence-based movement practices for anxiety; specific anxiety-reducing poses: forward folds (Paschimottanasana), legs-up-the-wall, child's pose, and gentle twists
- **Walking in nature** (forest bathing / Shinrin-yoku): 20-30 minutes significantly lowers cortisol and sympathetic nervous system activity
- Avoid high-intensity exercise during acute anxiety phases; it can temporarily spike cortisol and worsen symptoms
- **Tai chi and qigong**: meditative movement practices with strong evidence for anxiety reduction; combine breath, movement, and mindfulness
- Consistency matters more than intensity; 20-30 minutes of moderate movement daily outperforms sporadic intense workouts

### Mind & Stress
- The body keeps the score -- somatic therapies (somatic experiencing, EMDR, sensorimotor psychotherapy) address trauma stored in the nervous system that talk therapy alone cannot reach
- **Box breathing** (4-4-4-4) or physiological sigh (double inhale through nose, long exhale through mouth) for acute anxiety episodes
- **Vagus nerve stimulation:** humming, singing, gargling, and cold water face immersion activate the parasympathetic response within seconds
- **Grounding practices:** barefoot walking on earth, body scanning, 5-4-3-2-1 sensory technique
- **Heart Rate Variability (HRV) biofeedback** training builds vagal tone and resilience over 4-8 weeks of consistent practice
- Boundaries, rest, and reducing "obligation overload" are foundational -- anxiety often signals an overcommitted life

## Cross-Domain Connections
Anxiety management strongly benefits from the **Mind-Body Practitioner** (for somatic therapy, breathwork, meditation instruction, and vagal tone training), **Mental Health Counselor** (for CBT, EMDR, and therapeutic processing), and **Nutrition Therapist** (for gut-brain axis repair, blood sugar stabilization, and neurotransmitter precursor support). The **Herbalist** provides anxiolytic and adaptogenic botanicals. If gut dysbiosis is a driver, the **Functional Medicine** specialist can guide testing and targeted protocols.

## Red Flags
- Panic attacks that are increasing in frequency, severity, or duration
- Suicidal ideation or self-harm -- immediate psychiatric evaluation
- Substance use (alcohol, benzodiazepines) to manage anxiety with signs of dependence
- Anxiety accompanied by chest pain, shortness of breath, or syncope -- rule out cardiac conditions
- Social withdrawal to the point of inability to work, maintain relationships, or perform daily activities
- New onset of anxiety after age 50 or sudden severe onset -- may indicate thyroid dysfunction, cardiac issues, or neurological conditions
