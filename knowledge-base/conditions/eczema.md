# Eczema (Atopic Dermatitis)

## Holistic Perspective
Eczema is understood holistically as an external manifestation of internal imbalance -- primarily a dysfunction of the skin barrier combined with immune system overactivation. The concept of "leaky skin" mirrors "leaky gut": impaired barrier integrity allows allergens and microbes to penetrate, triggering chronic Th2-dominant inflammation. Root causes often include food sensitivities, intestinal permeability, nutrient deficiencies (especially essential fatty acids and zinc), environmental triggers, and stress. Healing eczema requires an inside-out approach that restores barrier integrity, calms immune overactivation, and addresses the gut-skin axis.

## Common Patterns
- Dry, itchy, inflamed, and red patches of skin; typically flexural areas (elbow creases, behind knees) in adults; face and body in infants
- Scratching cycle that worsens inflammation and can lead to skin thickening (lichenification) and secondary infection
- Co-occurring: asthma, allergic rhinitis (the "atopic triad"), food allergies/sensitivities, IBS, SIBO, environmental allergies, dry skin generally
- Lab markers: elevated IgE, elevated eosinophils, low vitamin D, low essential fatty acid profile, positive food sensitivity panels, elevated hs-CRP, low zinc, low secretory IgA (gut mucosal immunity), zonulin elevation (intestinal permeability)
- Body systems: integumentary (skin), immune system, digestive system (gut-skin axis), nervous system (stress-skin connection)

## Holistic Approaches

### Nutrition
- **Elimination diet** to identify trigger foods; common eczema triggers: dairy, eggs, gluten, soy, nuts, citrus, and food additives (eliminate for 3-4 weeks, reintroduce systematically)
- Omega-3 fatty acids: 2-4g EPA/DHA daily from fish oil or 1 tbsp cod liver oil; essential for anti-inflammatory eicosanoid production and skin barrier integrity
- **Probiotic-rich foods** daily: sauerkraut, kimchi, coconut yogurt, kefir -- specific strains (Lactobacillus rhamnosus GG, Bifidobacterium lactis) reduce eczema severity in clinical trials
- **Bone broth** (8-12 oz daily) for collagen, glycine, and amino acids that support skin repair and gut healing
- Increase zinc-rich foods: pumpkin seeds (1/4 cup daily), oysters, grass-fed beef, chickpeas (zinc deficiency impairs skin barrier repair)
- Vitamin C-rich foods: bell peppers, kiwi, strawberries, citrus -- essential for collagen synthesis and immune modulation
- **Quercetin-rich foods:** apples (with skin), red onions, capers, berries -- natural antihistamine and anti-inflammatory flavonoid
- Eliminate refined sugar and processed foods -- sugar directly feeds inflammation and Th2 immune dominance
- Evening primrose oil or borage oil (500-1000 mg GLA daily) for gamma-linolenic acid to support skin barrier lipids

### Herbal Medicine
- **Stinging Nettle (Urtica dioica):** 300-500 mg freeze-dried capsules 2-3x daily or 1-2 cups strong tea; natural antihistamine; safe for long-term use
- **Turmeric/Curcumin:** 500-1000 mg standardized extract with piperine daily; potent anti-inflammatory that reduces Th2 cytokines
- **Burdock Root (Arctium lappa):** 2-4 mL tincture 3x daily or as tea; traditional blood purifier and alterative that supports liver detoxification and skin clearance
- **Oregon Grape (Mahonia aquifolium):** topical cream (10% extract) and internal use (500 mg 2-3x daily); berberine content is anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial for secondary skin infections
- **Chamomile (Matricaria recutita):** topical cream or compress for acute inflammation; internal tea (2-3 cups daily) for systemic calming
- **Calendula (Calendula officinalis):** topical salve or oil for skin barrier repair and wound healing; anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial
- **Aloe vera:** pure gel applied topically 2-3x daily; anti-inflammatory, moisturizing, and promotes wound healing
- **Licorice Root (Glycyrrhiza glabra):** topical gel (2%) reduces redness and itching; internal use (250-500 mg) for systemic anti-inflammatory effect; avoid with high blood pressure

### Ayurvedic Perspective
- Primarily a **Pitta** imbalance (heat, redness, inflammation, itching, oozing) with **Vata** involvement (dryness, scaling, cracking) and sometimes **Kapha** (oozing, thickening, weeping)
- **Neem (Azadirachta indica):** internal (500 mg capsules 2x daily) and topical (neem oil or neem leaf paste); premier Ayurvedic herb for skin disorders; blood purifier and antimicrobial
- **Guduchi (Tinospora cordifolia):** 500-1000 mg daily; immunomodulator that reduces allergic response and supports liver detoxification
- **Manjistha (Rubia cordifolia):** 500 mg 2x daily; powerful blood purifier (rakta shodhak) specifically indicated for skin diseases in Ayurveda
- **Triphala** (1/2 tsp in warm water before bed) for gentle detoxification and gut support
- **Khadirarishta** (10-20 mL with water after meals): classical Ayurvedic fermented preparation for skin diseases
- Apply coconut oil infused with neem and turmeric topically; coconut oil is both moisturizing and antimicrobial
- Avoid Pitta-aggravating foods: spicy food, excess sour foods, alcohol, and excessive sun exposure

### Movement & Exercise
- **Swimming** in saltwater pools or ocean is excellent -- salt water is antimicrobial and healing for eczema; chlorine pools can be irritating (shower immediately after)
- Gentle walking and yoga preferred over sweat-inducing exercise; sweat can irritate and worsen eczema
- **Yin yoga or restorative yoga** to activate parasympathetic nervous system; stress directly triggers eczema flares via cortisol-immune pathways
- Avoid exercising in extreme temperatures (very hot or very cold) which can trigger flares
- Sun exposure (10-15 min daily) for vitamin D synthesis -- low vitamin D is strongly associated with eczema severity

### Mind & Stress
- **Stress is one of the most common eczema triggers** -- the skin and nervous system develop from the same embryonic layer (ectoderm) and remain deeply connected
- The itch-scratch cycle intensifies with anxiety; mindfulness and body awareness practices can interrupt the automatic response
- **Gut-directed hypnotherapy** has shown benefits for eczema through the gut-skin-brain axis
- Sleep disruption from nighttime itching creates a vicious cycle -- prioritize sleep optimization and consider silk pajamas or cotton gloves at night
- Body-based practices (somatic therapy, body scanning) help develop awareness of early flare signals and reduce reactive scratching

## Red Flags
- Signs of secondary skin infection: increasing pain, warmth, pus, yellow crusting, fever, or rapidly spreading redness (cellulitis or eczema herpeticum -- a serious viral complication)
- Eczema covering more than 20% of body surface area or failing to respond to holistic interventions after 3-6 months
- Severe sleep disruption affecting daily functioning and mental health
- Signs of allergic reaction progressing to anaphylaxis: swelling of lips/tongue, difficulty breathing, dizziness
- Use of topical steroids causing skin thinning, stretch marks, or withdrawal flares (topical steroid withdrawal syndrome)
- Eczema in infants under 6 months with failure to thrive or feeding difficulties
