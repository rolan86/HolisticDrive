# Hashimoto's Thyroiditis

## Holistic Perspective
Hashimoto's is understood holistically as an autoimmune condition rooted in a triad of genetic predisposition, environmental triggers, and intestinal permeability. The immune system mistakenly targets thyroid tissue, but the underlying drivers often originate in the gut, from chronic stress disrupting HPA axis function, and from exposure to environmental toxins and inflammatory foods. Healing focuses on modulating the immune response, reducing systemic inflammation, and addressing the root triggers rather than simply replacing thyroid hormone.

## Common Patterns
- Fatigue (especially morning), weight gain or difficulty losing weight, brain fog, cold intolerance, hair thinning, dry skin, constipation, depression
- Co-occurring conditions: celiac disease, SIBO, adrenal dysfunction, nutrient deficiencies (B12, iron, selenium, vitamin D), other autoimmune conditions
- Lab markers: elevated TPO and thyroglobulin antibodies, suboptimal or elevated TSH, low free T3, reverse T3 elevation, low selenium, low vitamin D (25-OH), elevated homocysteine, positive ANA
- Body systems: endocrine (thyroid, adrenal), immune system, digestive system, detoxification pathways

## Holistic Approaches

### Nutrition
- Strict gluten elimination -- molecular mimicry between gliadin and thyroid tissue is a well-documented trigger; even trace gluten can sustain antibody production
- Emphasize selenium-rich foods: 2-3 Brazil nuts daily (provide ~200 mcg selenium), wild salmon, eggs, sunflower seeds
- Include iodine cautiously from natural sources: seaweed (nori, dulse), cod, eggs; avoid high-dose iodine supplementation without testing as it can flare autoimmunity
- Increase zinc-rich foods: pumpkin seeds (1/4 cup daily provides ~50% RDA), oysters, grass-fed beef, chickpeas
- Anti-inflammatory omega-3 sources: wild-caught fatty fish 3-4x weekly, flaxseed oil, walnuts
- Cook with anti-inflammatory spices: turmeric (1/2 tsp daily with black pepper), ginger, rosemary
- Prioritize cruciferous vegetables cooked (not raw): broccoli, cauliflower, Brussels sprouts -- cooking deactivates goitrogens
- Bone broth daily (8-12 oz) for gut healing and collagen support; add vegetables and anti-inflammatory herbs

### Herbal Medicine
- **Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera):** 300-600 mg KSM-66 extract twice daily; supports thyroid hormone production (T3/T4) and modulates immune response; avoid in hyperthyroidism
- **Selenium** (as selenomethionine): 200 mcg daily; clinically shown to reduce TPO antibodies by 20-40% over 3-6 months
- **Bugleweed (Lycopus europaeus):** useful when there is a hyperthyroid swing; 1-2 mL tincture 3x daily -- short-term use only under practitioner guidance
- **Rosemary (Rosmarinus officinalis):** antioxidant and anti-inflammatory; use as tea (1 tsp dried herb steeped 10 min) or culinary amounts freely
- **Schisandra (Schisandra chinensis):** adaptogenic berry, 500-1000 mg extract daily; supports liver detoxification and HPA axis regulation
- **Guggul (Commiphora mukul):** 500 mg 2x daily of standardized extract; traditionally used to support thyroid function and lipid metabolism; caution with blood thinners
- **Turmeric/Curcumin:** 500-1000 mg standardized extract with piperine for systemic anti-inflammatory action
- **Reishi mushroom (Ganoderma lucidum):** 1000-2000 mg daily as an immune modulator; shift immune response from Th1/Th17 dominance toward regulation

### Ayurvedic Perspective
- Primarily a **Kapha** imbalance (sluggishness, weight, cold, swelling) with **Vata** involvement (fatigue, dryness, anxiety) and secondary **Pitta** (inflammation, autoimmunity)
- **Guggulu** preparations (Kaishore Guggulu, 500 mg 2x daily) are classical for thyroid support and meda-dhatu (fat tissue) metabolism
- Dry brushing (Garshana) with raw silk gloves before bathing stimulates lymphatic flow and supports Kapha reduction
- Favor warm, light, well-spiced foods; minimize dairy, wheat, soy, refined sugar, and excessive raw or cold foods
- **Trikatu** (ginger, black pepper, long pepper) 1/4 tsp with honey before meals to kindle digestive fire (Agni)
- Nasya therapy (oil application to nasal passages) with Anu taila or brahmi ghee to support the head and neck region

### Movement & Exercise
- Moderate-intensity exercise 30-45 minutes most days: brisk walking, swimming, cycling
- Avoid excessive cardio which can stress the HPA axis and worsen fatigue; more is not better
- Yoga: Sarvangasana (shoulder stand) and Matsyasana (fish pose) are traditionally believed to improve thyroid circulation (avoid inversions during heavy menstrual periods)
- Strength training 2-3x weekly supports metabolic rate and healthy body composition
- Listen to the body -- rest days are essential; push-through mentality worsens autoimmune patterns

### Mind & Stress
- Chronic stress directly suppresses thyroid function via elevated cortisol inhibiting T4-to-T3 conversion and increasing reverse T3
- **Adrenal support** is almost always needed alongside thyroid work; address cortisol rhythms before optimizing thyroid hormones
- **Trauma-informed practices:** autoimmune conditions often have links to chronic emotional stress, unresolved trauma, or chronic self-neglect patterns
- Breathwork: slow nasal breathing (5.5-second inhale, 5.5-second exhale, 5.5-second hold) for HPA axis regulation
- Adequate sleep (8-9 hours) is non-negotiable; thyroid repair happens during deep sleep phases

## Red Flags
- Rapidly enlarging thyroid or visible goiter, difficulty swallowing or breathing
- Severe fatigue that prevents daily functioning (may indicate myxedema or severe hypothyroidism)
- Heart palpitations, chest pain, or resting heart rate above 100 (suggests thyroid storm or over-replacement)
- Symptoms of myxedema: extreme cold intolerance, profound lethargy, edema, confusion
- New or worsening neurological symptoms: numbness, tingling, balance issues
- Pregnancy -- thyroid requirements change significantly and need close medical monitoring
