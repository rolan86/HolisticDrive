# PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome)

## Holistic Perspective
PCOS is understood holistically as a metabolic and endocrine disorder rooted in insulin resistance, chronic low-grade inflammation, and hormonal imbalance rather than merely a reproductive condition. The ovaries are downstream victims of broader systemic dysfunction -- elevated insulin drives excess androgen production, inflammation disrupts ovulation, and the adrenal-thyroid-hormone axis becomes dysregulated. A holistic approach targets the metabolic root (insulin resistance and inflammation) while supporting hormonal balance, liver detoxification, and stress modulation.

## Common Patterns
- Irregular or absent menstrual cycles, anovulation, difficulty conceiving, heavy or prolonged periods
- Androgen excess symptoms: hirsutism (facial/body hair growth), acne (especially jawline), male-pattern hair thinning
- Metabolic signs: weight gain (especially abdominal), difficulty losing weight, skin tags, acanthosis nigricans (darkened skin folds)
- Co-occurring: insulin resistance, type 2 diabetes risk, NAFLD, sleep apnea, anxiety, depression, endometrial hyperplasia
- Lab markers: elevated LH:FSH ratio (typically >2:1), elevated total and free testosterone, elevated DHEA-S, elevated fasting insulin (>10 mcIU/mL), elevated HOMA-IR, low SHBG, elevated AMH, dyslipidemia, elevated hs-CRP
- Body systems: endocrine (ovaries, adrenals, pancreas), metabolic system, digestive system (liver), skin, reproductive system

## Holistic Approaches

### Nutrition
- **Low glycemic index eating** is the single most impactful dietary intervention; prioritize blood sugar stability with every meal
- Pair carbohydrates with protein, fat, and fiber -- never eat carbs alone; example: apple with almond butter, not apple alone
- Prioritize inositol-rich foods: cantaloupe, citrus fruits, beans, lentils, and brown rice (or supplement with myo-inositol)
- Increase chromium-rich foods: broccoli, barley, oats, green beans, and brewer's yeast (chromium enhances insulin sensitivity)
- Anti-inflammatory omega-3s: wild-caught fatty fish 3-4x weekly, chia seeds (2 tbsp daily), walnuts, flaxseed (ground, 1-2 tbsp daily for lignans)
- Include cruciferous vegetables daily (broccoli, cauliflower, Brussels sprouts) to support estrogen metabolism via liver detoxification pathways
- Full-fat dairy has been associated with worse PCOS symptoms in some studies; consider a 30-day dairy elimination to assess
- Green tea (2-3 cups daily) and spearmint tea (2 cups daily) -- spearmint has anti-androgenic properties, reducing free testosterone by ~50% in trials

### Herbal Medicine
- **Inositol (Myo-inositol):** 2000-4000 mg daily; one of the most evidence-based supplements for PCOS; improves insulin sensitivity, ovulation rates, and egg quality; often combined with folic acid (400 mcg)
- **Berberine:** 500 mg 2-3x daily with meals; comparable to metformin for improving insulin sensitivity and lipid profiles in PCOS trials; caution with CYP3A4 medications
- **Spearmint (Mentha spicata):** 2 cups of strong tea daily or 300 mg extract; anti-androgenic -- reduces free testosterone and hirsutism
- **Vitex (Vitex agnus-castus / Chasteberry):** 20-40 mg daily of standardized extract; supports progesterone production via dopaminergic activity in the pituitary; take in the morning, cycle days 1-14; avoid with hormonal contraceptives
- **Saw Palmetto (Serenoa repens):** 320 mg daily; 5-alpha-reductase inhibitor that blocks conversion of testosterone to DHT; reduces hirsutism and acne
- **Cinnamon (Cinnamomum cassia):** 1-2 tsp daily in food or 500 mg capsules; improves insulin sensitivity and menstrual cyclicity
- **Licorice Root (Glycyrrhiza glabra):** 250-500 mg daily short-term; blocks testosterone production and supports adrenal health; avoid with high blood pressure
- **Peony (Paeonia lactiflora) + Licorice combination:** Traditional Chinese Medicine formula (Shakuyaku-kanzo-to) with strong evidence for lowering testosterone in PCOS

### Ayurvedic Perspective
- Primarily a **Kapha** imbalance (weight gain, cysts, fluid retention, sluggishness) with **Vata** involvement (irregular cycles, anxiety) and secondary **Pitta** (inflammation, acne)
- **Guggulu** preparations: Kaishore Guggulu (500 mg 2x daily) for hormonal balance and metabolic support; medohara guggulu for weight management
- **Shatavari (Asparagus racemosus):** 500-1000 mg or 1 tsp in warm milk daily; premier female reproductive tonic; supports fertility and balances estrogen
- **Dashamoola** (10-root decoction): for pelvic congestion and Kapha accumulation in the lower abdomen
- **Punarnava (Boerhavia diffusa):** 500 mg daily; diuretic and anti-inflammatory; reduces Kapha-type water retention and supports kidney function
- Favor light, warm, well-spiced foods; minimize dairy, sugar, wheat, and cold drinks
- Udvartana (herbal powder massage) -- dry, stimulating massage to reduce Kapha and support lymphatic drainage

### Movement & Exercise
- **Resistance training** 2-3x weekly is more effective than cardio alone for PCOS; building muscle improves insulin sensitivity more than aerobic exercise
- **HIIT (High-Intensity Interval Training):** 20-25 minutes 2x weekly improves insulin sensitivity and hormonal markers more than steady-state cardio
- Moderate walking (30-45 min daily) for baseline metabolic health and stress reduction
- Yoga: poses that open the hips and pelvis (Baddha Konasana, Supta Padangusthasana, Malasana) are traditionally believed to improve pelvic circulation
- Avoid over-exercising, which can further stress the HPA axis and worsen hormonal disruption

### Mind & Stress
- Cortisol directly worsens insulin resistance and androgen production; stress management is a core PCOS intervention, not optional
- **Sleep quality** profoundly impacts insulin sensitivity; 7-9 hours of quality sleep is as important as diet for PCOS management
- Mindfulness-based stress reduction (MBSR) has been shown to improve hormonal markers and reduce anxiety in PCOS patients
- Body image work is often needed -- PCOS symptoms (hirsutism, weight, acne) create significant psychological burden
- Community support (PCOS-specific support groups) reduces isolation and provides practical coping strategies

## Cross-Domain Connections
PCOS management strongly benefits from the **Nutrition Therapist** (for insulin resistance-focused nutrition plans and anti-inflammatory dietary protocols), **Functional Medicine** specialist (for comprehensive hormone and metabolic panels, insulin resistance testing, and targeted supplementation), and **Herbalist** (for hormone-modulating and insulin-sensitizing botanicals). The **Mind-Body Practitioner** addresses stress-hormone connections. For fertility goals, coordination with a reproductive endocrinologist is essential while maintaining holistic support.

## Red Flags
- Prolonged absence of menstruation (>3 months without hormonal contraception) -- increased endometrial hyperplasia and cancer risk
- Severe abdominal or pelvic pain with fever, nausea, or vomiting (possible ovarian torsion or rupture)
- Rapid unexplained weight gain with swelling in legs or face
- Signs of severe insulin resistance: fasting glucose >126 mg/dL, HbA1c >6.5%, acanthosis nigricans worsening
- Heavy vaginal bleeding with dizziness or fainting
- Difficulty conceiving after 12 months of regular unprotected intercourse (or 6 months if over 35)
