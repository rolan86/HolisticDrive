# Adrenal Fatigue (HPA Axis Dysregulation)

## Holistic Perspective
Often more accurately described as HPA (hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal) axis dysregulation, this pattern reflects a breakdown in the body's stress-response system after prolonged physical, emotional, or environmental stress. Rather than the adrenal glands literally being "exhausted," the brain's signaling becomes dysregulated, leading to abnormal cortisol rhythms, disrupted circadian rhythms, and widespread downstream effects on immunity, metabolism, and mood. A holistic approach addresses the stressors themselves, restores nervous system regulation, and rebuilds metabolic reserves.

## Common Patterns
- Fatigue that is worse in the morning (difficulty waking) or crashes mid-afternoon; feeling "wired but tired" at night
- Reliance on caffeine to function; sugar cravings, especially in the afternoon or evening
- Brain fog, poor concentration, difficulty with memory recall
- Salt cravings, light-headedness upon standing (orthostatic hypotension), low blood pressure
- Poor sleep quality despite exhaustion; waking between 2-4 AM
- Co-occurring: anxiety, mild depression, decreased libido, frequent infections, food sensitivities
- Lab markers: abnormal cortisol diurnal rhythm (DUTCH test or 4-point salivary cortisol), low DHEA-S, inverted cortisol curve (high evening, low morning), low pregnenolone, low morning testosterone, ferritin depletion
- Body systems: endocrine (HPA axis), nervous system, immune system, metabolic system

## Holistic Approaches

### Nutrition
- Stabilize blood sugar with balanced meals: protein + healthy fat + fiber at every meal; never eat carbohydrates alone
- Emphasize nutrient-dense foods for adrenal support: organ meats (liver 2-3x weekly), pastured eggs, bone broth, dark leafy greens
- Increase vitamin C-rich foods: bell peppers, kiwi, citrus, strawberries (adrenal glands have one of the highest vitamin C concentrations of any organ)
- Include magnesium-rich foods: dark chocolate (70%+, 1-2 squares), pumpkin seeds, spinach, black beans, avocados
- Sea salt (Celtic or Himalayan, 1/4 tsp in warm water upon waking) to support aldosterone function and blood pressure
- Adaptogenic mushroom teas: reishi, chaga, or lion's mane (1 tsp powder steeped 10 min)
- Eliminate or drastically reduce caffeine; if using, consume before 10 AM only and never on an empty stomach
- Collagen peptides (10-15g) in morning smoothie for amino acid support (glycine, proline)

### Herbal Medicine
- **Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera):** 300-600 mg KSM-66 extract twice daily; primary adaptogen for cortisol regulation; KSM-66 shown to lower cortisol by up to 30%
- **Rhodiola (Rhodiola rosea):** 200-400 mg standardized to 3% rosavins in the morning only; excellent for morning fatigue and cognitive support; avoid if anxiety is dominant
- **Licorice Root (Glycyrrhiza glabra):** 250-500 mg 2x daily before meals; extends half-life of cortisol to support morning levels; avoid with high blood pressure or longer than 6 weeks without supervision
- **Holy Basil / Tulsi (Ocimum sanctum):** 300-600 mg extract or 2-3 cups tea daily; gentle cortisol modulator with anxiolytic properties
- **Eleuthero (Siberian Ginseng):** 200-400 mg standardized extract in the morning; stamina and endurance support during recovery phase
- **Astragalus (Astragalus membranaceus):** 500-1000 mg daily for immune restoration; chronic stress depletes immune reserves
- **Schisandra (Schisandra chinensis):** 500-1000 mg daily; supports liver function, adrenal health, and mental clarity
- **Macuna pruriens (L-Dopa source):** 250-500 mg at bedtime if dopamine depletion is contributing to low motivation and cravings

### Ayurvedic Perspective
- Primarily a **Vata** depletion (exhaustion, anxiety, dryness, irregular rhythms) with secondary **Kapha** involvement (lethargy, weight gain, cravings) in later stages
- **Chyawanprash** (1-2 tsp daily) -- classical Ayurvedic rejuvenative jam containing amla (highest natural vitamin C source) and 40+ herbs for systemic rebuilding (rasayana)
- **Shatavari (Asparagus racemosus):** 500-1000 mg or 1 tsp powder in warm milk at bedtime; premier reproductive and adrenal tonic, especially for women
- **Brahmi (Bacopa monnieri):** 300-500 mg at bedtime; supports nervous system repair and cognitive function
- Daily oil massage (Abhyanga) with warm sesame oil or Bala oil to ground Vata and nourish depleted tissues
- Favor warm, sweet, grounding foods: sweet potatoes, ghee, rice, warm milk with nutmeg, dates, and almonds
- Establish consistent daily routine (Dinacharya) with regular sleep/wake times -- predictability is medicine for Vata

### Movement & Exercise
- **Restorative phase** (severe fatigue): gentle walking (10-15 min), restorative yoga, tai chi -- no heart rate elevation
- **Recovery phase** (improving energy): moderate walking (30 min), gentle swimming, yin yoga
- **Avoid:** high-intensity interval training, long-distance running, heavy weightlifting -- these are stressors the system cannot currently handle
- Morning sunlight exposure (10-15 min within first hour of waking) to reset circadian cortisol rhythm
- **Non-Sleep Deep Rest (NSDR)** or Yoga Nidra (20-30 min daily) for parasympathetic activation without physical demand

### Mind & Stress
- Identify and reduce or eliminate the chronic stressors driving HPA dysregulation -- this is the most important intervention
- **Boundaries and saying no** are therapeutic interventions, not optional lifestyle choices
- Cortisol is lowest between 10 PM and 2 AM; being asleep during this window is essential for repair
- Screen-free evenings (devices off by 8 PM) to protect melatonin production and sleep architecture
- **Heart Rate Variability (HRV) training** using biofeedback apps (e.g., Resonance breathing at 5.5 breaths/min) to build vagal tone
- Somatic experiencing or EMDR therapy if unresolved trauma is maintaining chronic sympathetic activation

## Cross-Domain Connections
HPA axis dysregulation strongly benefits from the **Mind-Body Practitioner** (for somatic therapy, breathwork, and nervous system regulation), **Nutrition Therapist** (for blood sugar stabilization and adrenal-supportive nutrition), and **Functional Medicine** specialist (for cortisol rhythm testing and hormone panel interpretation). The **Herbalist** provides adaptogenic protocols. If chronic stress is rooted in psychological patterns, the **Mental Health Counselor** should be part of the team.

## Red Flags
- Severe depression with suicidal ideation -- this requires immediate psychiatric care
- Blood pressure consistently below 90/60 with fainting episodes
- Inability to get out of bed for multiple days (may indicate more serious endocrine pathology)
- Unexplained rapid weight loss, night sweats, or swollen lymph nodes
- Symptoms persisting beyond 6-12 months of dedicated holistic intervention warrant medical workup for Cushing's syndrome, Addison's disease, or other endocrine pathology
