## Ashwagandha

| Drug Class | Severity | Key Drugs | Mechanism | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Thyroid meds | Moderate | Levothyroxine, liothyronine | Enhances thyroid hormone synthesis; may increase T3/T4 levels | Monitor TSH every 4-6 weeks; dose reduction of thyroid med may be needed |
| Blood thinners | Moderate | Warfarin, clopidogrel | Platelet aggregation inhibition; may potentiate anticoagulant effect | Monitor INR closely if combined; watch for bruising or bleeding |
| Blood pressure meds | Moderate | Lisinopril, amlodipine, losartan | Hypotensive additive effect; ashwagandha lowers systolic and diastolic BP | Monitor BP; may require dose adjustment of antihypertensive |
| SSRIs/SNRIs | Mild | Sertraline, fluoxetine, venlafaxine | Theoretical: serotonergic activity may augment SSRI effect | Monitor for serotonin syndrome symptoms (rare); caution with high doses |
| Diabetes meds | Moderate | Metformin, insulin, glipizide | Enhances insulin secretion and improves insulin sensitivity | Monitor blood glucose; risk of hypoglycemia; may need dose adjustment |
| Immunosuppressants | Moderate | Prednisone, cyclosporine, tacrolimus | Immunostimulant; enhances immune cell activity | May reduce effectiveness of immunosuppressants; avoid in transplant patients |

## Licorice Root

| Drug Class | Severity | Key Drugs | Mechanism | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Blood pressure meds | Severe | Amlodipine, lisinopril, furosemide, spironolactone | Glycyrrhizin inhibits 11-beta-HSD2, causing sodium retention, potassium loss, and hypertension | Avoid combination; can negate antihypertensive effect; risk of hypokalemia and hypertensive crisis |
| Blood thinners | Moderate | Warfarin | Glycyrrhizin may induce warfarin metabolism via CYP2C9/CYP3A4, reducing INR | Monitor INR closely; may need warfarin dose increase |
| Diabetes meds | Moderate | Metformin, insulin, glimepiride | Glycyrrhizin causes hypokalemia which impairs insulin secretion | Monitor potassium and blood glucose; hypokalemia may worsen glycemic control |
| Thyroid meds | Moderate | Levothyroxine | Glycyrrhizin may reduce T4 to T3 conversion via cortisol-mediated pathway | Monitor TSH and free T4; may need dose adjustment |
| SSRIs/SNRIs | Mild | Fluoxetine, sertraline | Potassium depletion may exacerbate SSRI-related QT prolongation risk | Monitor potassium; caution especially with other QT-prolonging drugs |
| Immunosuppressants | Mild | Prednisone, dexamethasone | Glycyrrhizin inhibits cortisol breakdown, potentiating glucocorticoid effect | Monitor for Cushingoid symptoms; may allow lower steroid dose |

## Notes on Evidence Quality

- **Well-documented (clinical/case reports):** Ashwagandha + thyroid meds; Licorice root + BP meds; Turmeric + blood thinners; Ginger + blood thinners
- **Moderate evidence (clinical trials + pharmacology):** Ashwagandha + immunosuppressants; Holy basil + diabetes/BP meds; Rhodiola + SSRIs
- **Limited evidence (animal/in-vitro + theoretical):** Several "Mild" interactions; these require clinical judgment and should not be dismissed but also not over-stated
- **Theoretical interactions** are noted as such in the table where applicable
