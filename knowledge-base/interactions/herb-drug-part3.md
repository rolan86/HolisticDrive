# Herb-Drug Interactions (Part 3: Ayurvedic Herbs)

## Triphala

| Drug Class | Severity | Key Drugs | Mechanism | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Blood thinners | Mild | Warfarin, Aspirin | Mild antiplatelet and antioxidant effects may potentiate anticoagulation | Monitor INR; no strong clinical evidence of significant interaction |
| Thyroid medications | Moderate | Levothyroxine | Haritaki (one of three fruits) may reduce T3/T4 levels; potential interference with thyroid hormone absorption | Separate administration by 2+ hours; monitor thyroid function |
| Diabetes medications | Moderate | Metformin, Insulin, Sulfonylureas | Enhances glycemic control; additive hypoglycemic risk | Monitor blood glucose; possible dose adjustment of antidiabetic drugs |
| Blood pressure medications | Mild | ACE inhibitors, ARBs, Diuretics | Mild diuretic and antioxidant effects; may enhance BP-lowering | Monitor blood pressure; generally safe |

## Shatavari

| Drug Class | Severity | Key Drugs | Mechanism | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Blood thinners | Mild | Warfarin, Heparin | Minor antiplatelet activity reported | Monitor; clinically insignificant in most cases |
| Blood pressure medications | Mild | Antihypertensives | May lower blood pressure via diuretic-like action | Monitor BP; adjust dose if symptomatic hypotension |
| Diabetes medications | Moderate | Insulin, Metformin, Oral hypoglycemics | May lower blood glucose; additive hypoglycemic effect | Monitor blood glucose levels; adjust diabetic medication as needed |
| Sedatives | Mild | Benzodiazepines, Barbiturates | May enhance CNS calming effects | Caution with concurrent use; monitor for excessive sedation |

## Guggulu

| Drug Class | Severity | Key Drugs | Mechanism | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Blood thinners | Moderate | Warfarin, Aspirin, Clopidogrel | Guggulsterone inhibits platelet aggregation; may increase bleeding risk | Monitor INR and bleeding signs; use with caution |
| Thyroid medications | Moderate | Levothyroxine, Methimazole | Guggulsterone enhances thyroid hormone production and increases T3/T4 levels; may reduce need for exogenous thyroid hormone | Monitor thyroid function tests closely; dose adjustment may be needed |
| Blood pressure medications | Mild | Beta-blockers, ACE inhibitors | May lower blood pressure through lipid metabolism effects | Monitor BP; generally well-tolerated |
| Diabetes medications | Moderate | Insulin, Sulfonylureas, Metformin | Hypoglycemic activity; may enhance blood sugar lowering | Monitor blood glucose; potential dose reduction needed |
| Immunosuppressants | Moderate | Cyclosporine, Tacrolimus | May alter immune modulation; potential interference with immunosuppressive efficacy | Use with caution; monitor therapeutic drug levels and clinical response |

## Brahmi (Gotu Kola / Bacopa)

| Drug Class | Severity | Key Drugs | Mechanism | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Blood thinners | Moderate | Warfarin, Aspirin, Clopidogrel | Bacopa may inhibit platelet aggregation; enhanced bleeding risk | Monitor for bruising and bleeding; check INR if on warfarin |
| Thyroid medications | Moderate | Levothyroxine | May enhance thyroid hormone activity; reported to increase T4 levels | Monitor thyroid function tests; adjust levothyroxine dose if needed |
| Sedatives | Moderate | Benzodiazepines, Barbiturates, Zolpidem | Enhances GABAergic activity; additive CNS depression and sedation | Caution with concurrent use; avoid driving or operating machinery |
| Blood pressure medications | Mild | ACE inhibitors, Calcium channel blockers | May enhance vasodilatory effects; mild additive hypotension | Monitor blood pressure; generally safe at standard doses |
| Immunosuppressants | Mild | Cyclosporine, Prednisone | Immunostimulant properties may counteract immunosuppressive therapy | Monitor clinical status; use with caution in transplant patients |
