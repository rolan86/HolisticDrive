# Herb-Drug Interactions (Part 2: Nervine & Immune Herbs)

> **Scope:** Passionflower, valerian, chamomile, echinacea, reishi, astragalus vs. sedatives/benzodiazepines, SSRIs/SNRIs, blood thinners, immunosuppressants, diabetes medications, birth control/HRT.
>
> **Severity scale:** Mild = monitor; Moderate = caution + monitoring; Severe = avoid or strict medical supervision.
>
> **Disclaimer:** This is for informational purposes only and does not replace professional medical advice.

---

## Passionflower

| Drug Class | Severity | Key Drugs | Mechanism | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sedatives/Benzodiazepines | Moderate | Diazepam, lorazepam, zolpidem, buspirone | Additive CNS depression via GABAergic modulation | Use caution; may potentiate sedation and drowsiness |
| SSRIs/SNRIs | Mild | Sertraline, fluoxetine, venlafaxine | Mild MAOI-like activity may theoretically increase serotonin risk | Monitor for increased serotonergic effects; generally low risk |

---

## Valerian

| Drug Class | Severity | Key Drugs | Mechanism | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sedatives/Benzodiazepines | Moderate | Diazepam, lorazepam, zolpidem, alprazolam | Additive CNS depression; valerenic acid binds GABA-A receptors | Use caution; may increase sedation, impair motor function |
| SSRIs/SNRIs | Mild | Sertraline, paroxetine, duloxetine | Theoretical additive sedation; no direct pharmacokinetic interaction | Monitor for increased drowsiness |

## Chamomile

| Drug Class | Severity | Key Drugs | Mechanism | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Blood Thinners | Moderate | Warfarin, clopidogrel, aspirin | Coumarin constituents have mild anticoagulant activity; additive bleeding risk | Use caution; monitor INR if on warfarin, watch for bruising or bleeding |
| Sedatives/Benzodiazepines | Mild | Diazepam, zolpidem, lorazepam | Mild apigenin-mediated anxiolytic effect may enhance sedation | Monitor for increased drowsiness |

## Echinacea

| Drug Class | Severity | Key Drugs | Mechanism | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Immunosuppressants | Moderate | Cyclosporine, tacrolimus, methotrexate, prednisone | Stimulates immune function, opposing pharmacological immunosuppression | Avoid concurrent use; may reduce efficacy of transplant/rejection prevention |
| SSRIs/SNRIs | Mild | Sertraline, fluoxetine, citalopram | Echinacea inhibits CYP1A2 and CYP3A4, potentially altering drug metabolism | Monitor; dose adjustments of CYP1A2/CYP3A4 substrates may be needed |
| Birth Control/HRT | Mild | Ethinyl estradiol, levonorgestrel, conjugated estrogens | CYP3A4 inhibition may increase estrogen levels | Low clinical significance; monitor for side effects if used long-term |

## Reishi

| Drug Class | Severity | Key Drugs | Mechanism | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Blood Thinners | Moderate | Warfarin, clopidogrel, heparin, aspirin | Inhibits platelet aggregation; may potentiate anticoagulant effect | Use caution; monitor INR and watch for signs of excessive bleeding |
| Immunosuppressants | Moderate | Cyclosporine, tacrolimus, mycophenolate | Immunostimulatory polysaccharides (beta-glucans) counteract suppression | Avoid concurrent use; may compromise immunosuppressive therapy |
| Diabetes Medications | Mild | Metformin, insulin, glipizide, glyburide | May lower blood glucose through enhanced insulin sensitivity | Monitor blood glucose; potential for additive hypoglycemic effect |
| Blood Pressure Medications | Mild | Amlodipine, lisinopril, losartan | May add mild antihypertensive effect via ACE-inhibiting peptides | Monitor blood pressure; low risk of clinically significant interaction |

## Astragalus

| Drug Class | Severity | Key Drugs | Mechanism | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Immunosuppressants | Moderate | Cyclosporine, tacrolimus, azathioprine, prednisone | Activates T-cells, macrophages, and NK cells; directly opposes immunosuppression | Avoid concurrent use; may reduce efficacy of anti-rejection and autoimmune therapy |
| Blood Thinners | Mild | Warfarin, aspirin, clopidogrel | May have mild antiplatelet activity | Monitor for additive bleeding risk; clinical significance uncertain |
| Diabetes Medications | Mild | Metformin, insulin, glimepiride | Polysaccharides may enhance glucose uptake and lower blood sugar | Monitor blood glucose; watch for additive hypoglycemic effect |
| Blood Pressure Medications | Mild | Amlodipine, lisinopril, hydrochlorothiazide | Mild diuretic and vasodilatory effects | Monitor blood pressure; generally well-tolerated |
