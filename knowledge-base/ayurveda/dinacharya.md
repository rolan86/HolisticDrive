# Dinacharya: Daily Routine for Optimal Health

Dinacharya is the Ayurvedic practice of aligning daily habits with natural biological rhythms. Following a consistent routine stabilizes all three doshas, strengthens agni, and prevents disease. The schedule follows the dosha clock: Kapha (6-10), Pitta (10-14, 22-2), Vata (2-6, 14-18).

---

## Morning Routine (Brahma Muhurta Onward)

### 1. Rising -- Before 6:00 AM (ideally during Brahma Muhurta, ~90 min before sunrise)

**What:** Wake before 6 AM; Kapha time ends at 6 and sleeping into Kapha creates lethargy
**Why:** The pre-dawn hours carry sattvic (pure) energy; mind is clearest; cortisol naturally rises
**Dosha modifications:**
- Vata: may wake closer to 5:30 AM; too early in cold/dark aggravates Vata
- Pitta: wake by 5:30 AM; natural early risers; avoid staying in bed after waking
- Kapha: wake by 5:00 AM or earlier; sleeping past 6 AM is the single worst habit for Kapha

### 2. Elimination and Hygiene

**What:** Evacuate bowels, urinate, drink warm water
**Why:** Natural urge suppression leads to toxin (ama) accumulation; warm water stimulates peristalsis
**Dosha modifications:**
- Vata: add ghee or sesame oil to warm water to lubricate the gut
- Pitta: room-temperature or cool water; add cumin/fennel seeds
- Kapha: hot water with honey, lemon, or ginger; honey must not be heated above 40C

### 3. Tongue Scraping (Jihva Nirlekhana)

**What:** Scrape the tongue 7-14 times with a copper or stainless steel scraper
**When:** After brushing teeth, before eating or drinking
**Why:** Removes ama (toxic coating); improves taste perception; stimulates digestive organs via nerve reflex
**Dosha modifications:**
- All doshas: use copper (Kapha/Pitta) or gold/silver (Vata-sensitive) scrapers
- Observe coating: white = Kapha ama; yellow/green = Pitta ama; brown/gray = Vata ama

### 4. Oil Pulling (Gandusha / Kavala)

**What:** Swish 1 tbsp sesame or coconut oil in the mouth for 10-15 minutes, then spit
**When:** After tongue scraping, on an empty stomach
**Why:** Pulls toxins from oral mucosa; strengthens gums; reduces oral bacteria; improves jawline
**Dosha modifications:**
- Vata: warm sesame oil (warm in mouth before swishing)
- Pitta: coconut oil (cooling)
- Kapha: sunflower oil or sesame oil; add a drop of clove or tea tree oil

### 5. Nasya (Nasal Oil Application)

**What:** Instill 3-5 drops of medicated oil (Anu taila, Brahmi ghee, or plain sesame/ghee) into each nostril
**When:** After oil pulling, while tilting head back slightly
**Why:** Clears sinuses; nourishes brain and sensory organs; prevents headaches and allergies; directly pacifies Prana Vata
**Dosha modifications:**
- Vata: sesame oil or Brahmi ghee (warming, grounding)
- Pitta: Anu taila or coconut-based nasal oil
- Kapha: dry powder nasya (Vacha, Trikatu) if excess mucus; otherwise medicated oil

### 6. Abhyanga (Self-Massage with Oil)

**What:** Apply warm oil to entire body; massage in long strokes on limbs, circular on joints; leave 15-20 min then shower
**When:** Before bathing, ideally 20-30 min before shower
**Why:** Softens skin; moves lymph; calms nervous system; grounds Vata; pacifies all doshas when done correctly
**Dosha modifications:**
- Vata: warm sesame oil; daily; use generous amount; long, slow strokes; extra attention to feet, lower back, head
- Pitta: cool coconut oil or sunflower oil; 3-4x per week; moderate pressure; avoid head massage with oil (use Brahmi oil instead)
- Kapha: dry brush first (Garshana), then light mustard oil or sunflower oil; 2-3x per week; vigorous, stimulating strokes

### 7. Bathing (Snana)

**What:** Bathe with warm (not hot) water
**When:** After abhyanga oil has absorbed (15-20 min)
**Why:** Removes excess oil; cleanses pores; refreshes the body; shifts energy
**Dosha modifications:**
- Vata: warm water; avoid excessively hot water (drying)
- Pitta: lukewarm to cool water; never scalding
- Kapha: warm to hot water; use stimulating soaps (neem, eucalyptus)

### 8. Pranayama (Breathwork) -- 10-20 minutes

**What:** Sit comfortably; practice specific breathing techniques
**When:** After bathing, before or as part of meditation
**Why:** Balances nervous system; clears pranic channels; sets mental tone for the day
**Dosha modifications:**
- Vata: Nadi Shodhana (alternate nostril breathing) -- balancing, grounding; slow, deep Ujjayi breath
- Pitta: Sheetali or Sheetkari (cooling breaths); Chandravedha (left nostril breathing)
- Kapha: Bhastrika (bellows breath) -- stimulating; Kapalabhati (skull-shining breath) -- clears Kapha
- All doshas: Nadi Shodhana is universally beneficial; practice 5-10 rounds minimum

### 9. Exercise (Vyayama) -- 20-60 minutes

**What:** Physical activity appropriate to dosha and season
**When:** After pranayama, before breakfast; ideally before 8 AM (end of Kapha time)
**Why:** Moves stagnation; kindles agni; builds strength; clears channels
**Dosha modifications:**
- Vata: 50% of capacity; yoga, walking, light swimming; avoid wind and cold environments
- Pitta: 75% of capacity; swimming, cycling, skiing; avoid midday heat; competitive sports with awareness
- Kapha: 100% of capacity; running, HIIT, vigorous vinyasa yoga, dancing; must sweat daily

**Sign of right amount of exercise:** Breathing through the nose; feel energized, not exhausted. If breathing through mouth, reduce intensity.

### 10. Meditation -- 10-20 minutes

**What:** Sit quietly; observe breath or use mantra; cultivate inner stillness
**When:** After exercise, before starting the day's activities
**Why:** Reduces stress hormones; improves focus and emotional regulation; builds ojas (vital essence)
**Dosha modifications:**
- Vata: guided visualization, grounding mantras (Lam, Sham), focus on navel or root
- Pitta: heart-centered meditation, loving-kindness (metta), cooling imagery (moonlight, water)
- Kapha: stimulating practices, walking meditation, uplifting mantras (Om, Ram)

## Midday Routine

### Largest Meal at Noon (10:00 AM - 2:00 PM, Pitta Peak)

**What:** Eat the heaviest, most complete meal of the day
**When:** Between 11:30 AM and 1:30 PM (Pitta peak ensures strongest agni)
**Why:** Sun is at zenith; Pitta digestive fire is maximum; food is digested most efficiently
**Dosha modifications:**
- Vata: warm, moist, grounding meals; include ghee; well-cooked vegetables and grains
- Pitta: moderate meal; include bitter greens; avoid excessive spice; eat in a calm environment
- Kapha: lightest of the three doshas' noon meals; smallest grain portion; largest vegetable portion; skip dessert

### Brief Rest (Post-Meal)

**What:** Sit quietly for 10-15 minutes after eating; avoid lying down
**When:** Immediately after lunch
**Why:** Allows blood flow to digestive organs; prevents indigestion
**Dosha modifications:**
- Vata: lie on the LEFT side for 10 minutes (stomach on left aids digestion)
- Pitta: sit upright; walk slowly for 5-10 minutes
- Kapha: walk briskly for 10-15 minutes; absolutely no lying down

### Afternoon Work Block

**What:** Most productive mental work during 10 AM - 2 PM (Pitta time -- sharp intellect)
**Why:** Pitta governs discrimination, focus, and analytical thinking
**Dosha modifications:**
- Vata: take breaks every 90 min; avoid multitasking; warm herbal tea
- Pitta: leverage peak focus; set boundaries to avoid overworking; cool drinks
- Kapha: stand and move periodically; stimulating work; avoid sedentary posture

## Evening Routine

### Light Dinner (6:00 PM - 7:30 PM)

**What:** Eat a light, easily digestible meal; smaller than lunch
**When:** 3+ hours before bedtime (ideally by 7:00 PM)
**Why:** Digestive fire weakens after sunset; undigested food creates ama
**Dosha modifications:**
- Vata: warm soup, kitchari, stewed vegetables; avoid raw salads at night
- Pitta: grains, steamed vegetables, mild spices; avoid tomato, garlic, onion
- Kapha: broth, steamed vegetables, very light grains (barley, millet); skip second helpings

### Wind-Down Period (7:30 PM - 9:00 PM)

**What:** Reduce sensory stimulation; dim lights; disconnect from screens
**When:** 1-2 hours before bed
**Why:** Signals the body to begin melatonin production; calms Vata; prepares for deep sleep
**Dosha modifications:**
- Vata: warm milk with nutmeg and ghee; foot massage with warm sesame oil; calming music
- Pitta: cool chamomile or rose tea; gentle stretching; journaling to release the day
- Kapha: light reading; avoid heavy meals; brief walk after dinner

### Sleep Hygiene (Before 10:00 PM)

**What:** Be in bed by 10:00 PM; lights out
**When:** 10:00 PM latest (Pitta time 10 PM - 2 AM; if awake, the "second wind" is Pitta rising and will prevent sleep)
**Why:** The hours before midnight are twice as restorative; Pitta time from 10-2 performs internal repair
**Dosha modifications:**
- Vata: 9:30 PM bedtime; warm bath before bed; lavender or vetiver essential oil
- Pitta: 10:00 PM bedtime; cool bedroom (65-68F/18-20C); left nostril breathing before sleep
- Kapha: 10:00 PM latest; avoid sleeping more than 8 hours; set alarm for before 6 AM
